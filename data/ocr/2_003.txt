This study book is for:

・those who are seriously studying for the new JLPT
Level N3,

・have finished the beginners' level and wish to get ready
for the intermediate level

*・ those who wish to be able to read longer passages

をThe special features of this book

*・ You will study without much difficulty because you
will start with relatively easy spoken language and then
move up to N3 level reading,

* You will learn skills for searching for information and
understanding main ideas、 as well as key gramnar (1.e.
conjunctive expressions, demonstratives, functional
words, polite language etc.), which will be useful for
understanding the Dassages you read,

* The inclusion of a weekly practice test wi enable you
to regularly check your learning,

・The English, Vietnamese translations are included for
difficult sentences and words, which will enable you to
study alone.

Ler's enjoy learning!
